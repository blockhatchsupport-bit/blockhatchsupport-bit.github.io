Questions and answers

Is Block Hatch free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. There are optional purchases (No Ads, coins, packs and the Golden Egg) but you never need them to finish levels.

When can I download it?

Block Hatch is in its last testing weeks on Google Play. Follow us on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook to hear the day it opens for everyone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Levels, the daily challenge, your pals and your progress all work without internet. Only the online parts need a connection: leaderboards, weekly leagues and loading prices for purchases. When you are back online they work again.

Are there ads?

Ads are only shown between games, never during a move, and not on your first day. Videos that give rewards (like +5 moves) are always your choice. No Ads removes the ads between games forever.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login. Your progress is saved on your phone. Leaderboards show a pal nickname (like Pip 123) that you can change.

How do I hatch pals?

Clear lines to fill your egg. Every full egg hatches a pal: 6 common, 4 rare and 2 legendary pals. Each pal has its own power and skill, and twins help a pal grow.

Who is the game for?

Block Hatch is made for teens and adults (13+) who like relaxing puzzle games.

Is it on iPhone?

Android comes first. An iPhone version may follow later.

I bought something and did not get it. What do I do?

Open the shop and tap Restore purchases. If it is still missing, email us with the time of the purchase and we will fix it.
