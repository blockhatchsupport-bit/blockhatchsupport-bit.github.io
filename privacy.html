Block Hatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Developer: Block Hatch Games (Mohamed Fares Anwar Elhati), United Arab Emirates

This policy explains what information the Block Hatch game ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is published by an individual developer ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your real name, email, phone number or contacts.

Your game progress (board, levels, coins, collected pals, outfits, power-ups, missions, daily gift streak and settings) is saved on your own device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

2. Leaderboards and game statistics

The game uses Google Firebase, a service from Google:

  • Leaderboards. When you finish a level or a classic game, the game sends your score to Firebase so it can appear on the leaderboards. Along with the score we store a random player ID created by the game, the nickname you see in the leaderboard (you can change it at any time), your buddy pal and your supporter level. Your nickname and score are visible to other players. Please do not use your real name as a nickname.
  • Game statistics. Firebase Analytics records how the game is used (for example levels started and won, ads watched and items bought in the game) together with device type, operating system, app version and approximate country. We use this only to improve the game and its balance. The game does not send your advertising identifier to Firebase Analytics.
  • Game settings. The game may download updated game settings (for example reward amounts, events and which features are switched on) from Firebase.
  • Error reports. If the game hits an error, it sends a short technical message (the error text, where in the game it happened and the app version) through Firebase Analytics so we can fix it. It contains no personal information.
  • Invite codes. Each player has a short invite code made from the random player ID. If you enter a friend's code, we store that code together with your random player ID and the time, so both of you can receive the reward. Invite records do not contain names or contact details.
  • Leagues and daily challenges. Weekly league points and daily challenge scores are stored like other leaderboard scores (random player ID, nickname, score, buddy pal and supporter level).

Google's privacy information for Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy. To remove your leaderboard entries, email us your nickname.

3. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device type, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads.

Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's privacy settings. You can also reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your phone's settings.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are optional. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store using the payment methods you choose there. We never receive your card number, bank details or billing address.

When you buy an item, the store sends us a purchase confirmation (product, order or transaction ID, purchase time and status) so the game can deliver the item and prevent fraud. The game checks each confirmation with Google Play (or the App Store) before delivering the item.

Subscriptions. The Supporter Club is an optional monthly subscription. It renews automatically each month until you cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions (or in your Apple ID settings). Cancelling stops the next renewal; the benefits stay active until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store under their refund policies.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules notifications on your own device (for example when your daily gift or an egg is ready). They are created on your phone; no notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in the game settings or in your phone settings.

5. Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the advertising, store, Firebase and purchase verification providers described above, and when required by law.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to withdraw consent. Most of your game data stays on your device. For leaderboard entries or any other request, contact us and we will help. Step-by-step deletion instructions: blockhatchsupport-bit.github.io/delete-data.html.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the game and its accounts. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
