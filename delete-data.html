Delete your Block Hatch data

App: Block Hatch · Developer: Block Hatch Games (Mohamed Fares Anwar Elhati), United Arab Emirates

Block Hatch has no accounts. You can ask us to delete the data linked to you at any time, free of charge.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to blockhatch.support@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Block Hatch data.
  2. Include the nickname you use on the Block Hatch leaderboards (you can see it on the leaderboard screen in the game). This lets us find your entries.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.

What we delete

  • All your leaderboard entries: nickname, scores, random player ID, buddy pal and supporter level.
  • Any messages you sent us, once your request is complete.

Data you can delete yourself

  • Game progress (levels, coins, pals, outfits, settings) is stored only on your phone. Uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's settings to delete it.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).

Data that is kept

  • Game statistics (Firebase Analytics) are not linked to your nickname and are deleted automatically when the retention period set in Firebase ends (no longer than 14 months).
  • Purchase records (product, order ID, date) are kept only as long as needed for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention, as the law requires. Payments themselves are handled by Google Play.
