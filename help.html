Block Hatch help

App: Block Hatch · Developer: Block Hatch Games, United Arab Emirates · Contact: blockhatch.support@gmail.com

Where is my progress saved?

Block Hatch has no accounts. Your coins, levels, pals and settings are saved on your phone only, and the game keeps an automatic backup copy on the phone in case a save is damaged.

  • Closing the game, restarting the phone or updating the game keeps your progress.
  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes your progress and it cannot be brought back, also not by us. Please avoid "Clear data" if you want to keep playing where you were.
  • A new phone starts a new game. Items you bought that last forever (see below) can be restored on the new phone.

I bought something. How do I get it back?

Purchases are made and charged by Google Play, and the game checks each one with Google before it is added.

  • Items that last forever (like Remove Ads or supporter perks): open the Shop and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Google account you used to buy.
  • Items that are used up (like coin packs): these are added once and cannot be restored after they are spent, or after the game is uninstalled.
  • If Google says a payment is still pending, the item arrives automatically as soon as Google confirms it. You can keep playing in the meantime.

Refunds and payment questions

Because Google Play handles the payment, refunds are requested from Google. Open play.google.com → Order history, find the Block Hatch order and choose Request a refund, or read Google Play's refund help. Google decides on refunds under its own policy. We cannot see or change your card details, and we will never ask for them.

A reward or purchase did not arrive

  1. Check your internet connection, then close and open the game again.
  2. For a purchase: open the Shop and tap Restore purchases.
  3. Still missing? Email us with the details below. We look at every message and reply as soon as we can.

What to include when you email us

  • Your Player ID (bottom of the Settings screen in the game) and the game version shown next to it.
  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • What happened, and roughly when. A screenshot helps, but please hide any personal details before you send it.
  • For a purchase: the Google Play order number (starts with GPA.), from your Google receipt email. Never send card numbers or passwords.
